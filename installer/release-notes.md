## MapleSyrup — your MapleStory buddy that watches the game with you and talks

MapleSyrup watches your MapleStory window and talks with you through your phone like a friend sitting next to you: ask how you're doing, how long until you level, how to get somewhere; it warns you when HP or MP runs low and cheers when you level up. Its dog — the chow chow from the logo — sits over the game and lives on your phone.

With an OpenAI API key it talks like ChatGPT in a natural voice, **sees your screen**, finds your HUD by itself (any resolution or layout) and **learns what you teach it** by talking: "see that? that's an Orange Mushroom — tell me when one shows up", "warn me when the boss is under 20%", "I'm level 70", "remember that my boss key is F10".

**New in 0.9: it coaches, it sees for itself, and it updates itself.** You play, it talks: it watches the game go by and speaks up when there is something worth saying — a new map, a level-up, EXP that hasn't moved, something on screen worth a callout — and keeps quiet while anyone talks ("stop coaching" turns it off). Syrup, the vision engine, now does every pixel of the work: the HUD found from the pixels alone, HP/MP/EXP measured every frame, the game's own digits read in its own font (no OCR per frame, no model on the per-frame path), objects you taught it followed with far less work per frame — and it holds up on a lava map. The phone page is minimal: the dog, what it says, Start listening, one line about the game; Settings (the voice, with a search) and Details behind the gear. And from now on it updates itself: a new version is fetched in the background, verified against the release key built in, and installed the next time it starts, with the previous version kept to go back to if the new one does not come up.

**Also in 0.9, a warning once per fight.** A beating and a low bar are each said once in a fight. Answer one with a potion and the beating is not shouted again in that fight, and the low bar not until the fight is over or the bar goes lower. A low bar nothing answers is said again after 12 seconds, then asked about ("25 percent HP. Are you going to pot or not?"), and after four warnings with no answer — not a word, no potion (HP or MP), no EXP — it stops: "Okay. No more pot talk from me." if you spoke lately, "you're away" words only to a room that has been silent for ten minutes. It holds the rest until you're back, you pot ("There it is.") or ten minutes pass, and the coach holds its tongue with it; a death and a level-up are said all the same. Pot within three seconds of the shout three fights running and it stops shouting "back off": it watches the fall instead, and shouts only when HP goes deeper than you usually let it, when the potion is late, or when HP goes under half the warning level — it never stays quiet near the bottom of the bar. A reading has to hold: one odd frame is neither a potion nor a bar to fall from, and a bar it can only guess at is not warned from until it settles. A bar it reads as low for minutes while your EXP keeps coming is a bar it is reading wrong: it says so once, and holds that bar's warnings until it reads above the warning level again. "I'll warn you sooner" comes only after a death it really did not warn about, with time to have warned. A level-up is still the number at the bottom left going up by one, for the same character, and a thing you taught it that keeps "appearing" is called out less and less.

**A warning is shouted, news is told.** A death, a level-up, the coach's word come at its usual pace; on the phone a warning is red and news amber, and the dog barks at warnings only. Its warnings, deaths and level-ups come in six or seven wordings for each voice, dealt like a deck: never the same twice running, in another order each night. A fight's first line never says "still" or "again", and a death says "told you to pot" only when it did. Its own lines have a lane of their own, so a warning never waits for it to think; and it knows what it said: with a key, "yeah yeah, I'm potting" is understood, and "Sure thing, boss." is no longer cut to "Boss." On a live call it hands its lines to the call at most every 20 seconds, with the number only for HP and MP; a death or a level-up goes at once and is said even if it comes late. A glance at another app costs nothing; when a sleeping phone drops the call, its lines go back to its own voice within seconds, the one it was about to hand over included. It says hello once per visit, in the call's voice or its own, whichever will talk, and on the phone only after your tap — never twice after a reload; a player it doesn't know yet hears what it does. Clips that waited for your first tap are played as news only if they still are. It asks "you still there?" only when the game has sat idle twenty minutes, and with a key it can welcome you back after a long silence. The coach reacts — a close call, a third death in ten minutes — and looks in less often the more you talk. Two Hebrew sentences on a call switch the phone's own listening to Hebrew, shown beside the language picker (now on the main screen) with an × to undo it. "Don't talk to me this way" drops the attitude to friendly, on a live call too.

**And it talks like a friend, not a status bar.** "Hey" gets a "hey" back at once, never silence; before the game is open it just talks; it doesn't recite your level and map unless you ask; and "talk to me in Hebrew" switches it — its words, the phone's language and what the phone listens for — at once. On MapleStory Classic World it reads the HUD's own numbers too (HP[178/178] above the bar), so its warnings rest on what the game prints, not on a guess at the bar.

**New in 0.8: its own voice, and the dog comes alive.** With an ElevenLabs key, pick the voice it speaks in on the phone, from the voices on your account (Hebrew too, with Eleven v4); OpenAI's voice steps in whenever ElevenLabs can't. The dog is now the chow chow from the MapleSyrup logo, with a body and paws, in a box of its own at the top of the phone page: it hops about, runs, jumps, spins, rolls over, sniffs, scratches, plays with its ball and naps in its bed as it likes; it looks at you while you talk, tilts its head while it thinks, barks at danger, jumps for joy at a level up and plays dead when you die. Its mouth opens with the voice, as loud as it is. Tap the box and it comes running; tap the dog to pet it.

**New in 0.7: a real gaming buddy.** One short sentence, the point first, no "let me check": it answers right away and checks facts in the background, speaking again only if it was wrong. It's bossy, and you pick how it talks to you on the phone: *Friendly*, *Blunt* (the usual) or *Savage* (swears and roasts you, personally). Your own numbers ("how much HP do I have", "how long to level") are answered instantly, without a model. With an xAI key, **Grok** answers the conversation (faster and freer), with OpenAI as the voice and the backup.

**New in 0.6: it learns as you play.** It keeps a notebook about you — your characters, what you're working towards, how you like it to talk, the names you use, what you did last time — and picks up from there next time. It answers faster, straight from what it knows (a quick best guess rather than a search); correct it ("no, Easy Zakum is level 50") and it keeps the right version for good. It waits less after you stop talking, and adapts: longer if you pause mid-sentence, shorter answers if you talk over long ones, sooner HP warnings after a death it didn't warn you about, or the warnings you ask for ("warn me at 40%"). The phone shows what it knows about you, with Forget on each thing; it all stays on your PC.

It talks like a person, on a live call like ChatGPT's voice mode: speak any language and switch or mix languages mid-sentence, talk over it and it stops and goes with what you said, and it hears you while it talks (its voice comes from your phone or earbuds; or choose the PC speakers).

**It records the session for you:** tap *Record the session* on the phone (or say "start recording") and MapleSyrup saves a video of the whole screen with every sound — the game, its voice and yours — in the session folder, everything in sync.

It speaks your language: the phone page comes in 14 languages (English, עברית, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch, 한국어, 日本語, 简体中文, 繁體中文, ไทย, Tiếng Việt, Bahasa Indonesia, Русский), it listens in the one you pick, and it answers in the language you talk to it.

### Install
1. Download **MapleSyrup-Setup-….exe** below and run it. It installs for your user only (no administrator), adds MapleSyrup to the Start menu and the desktop, and asks for an OpenAI API key (optional; you can add it later).
2. Start MapleSyrup and MapleStory (windowed or borderless windowed).
3. Scan the QR code in MapleSyrup's window with your phone (same Wi-Fi), tap **Start listening**, and just talk.

No installer? **MapleSyrup-…-portable.zip** has the same program: unzip it anywhere and double-click MapleSyrup.exe.

### Good to know
- **Windows 10 (1903) or 11, 64-bit.** Nothing else to install: the voice, the text reading and `curl` come with Windows.
- **The program is not code-signed yet.** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": click *More info → Run anyway*. If *Smart App Control* is on, Windows blocks unsigned programs entirely; it can be turned off in Windows Security → App & browser control.
- **The phone link** is HTTPS on your local network with a certificate made on your PC, so the phone warns the page is "not private" (iPhone: *Show Details → visit this website*). Allow MapleSyrup on private networks if Windows asks. Or use **MapleSyrup (phone over the internet)** from the Start menu: no warning, works on mobile data.
- **Recording:** the first recording downloads ffmpeg once (about 150 MB). **MapleSyrup (recording and streaming)** in the Start menu records from the start, and shows the dog in OBS for streaming (use *Display Capture*).
- **Privacy:** your OpenAI key stays on your PC (`%APPDATA%\MapleSyrup`). With a key, what you say to MapleSyrup and pictures of your game window are sent to OpenAI to answer you (OpenAI charges your account for it: roughly cents per hour). What it learns about you is kept on your PC only (`memory.json` and `knowledge.json` there; delete them to make it forget). With an xAI key, your words and a small picture of the game go to xAI (Grok) for the regular replies; with an ElevenLabs key, what it says goes to ElevenLabs to be spoken. Without a key, nothing leaves your PC except the phone link on your own network.
- Sessions (a log of what was said, marked moments, recordings, `hud-found.png`) are kept in *Documents\MapleSyrup sessions*.
